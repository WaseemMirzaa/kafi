Delete your Kafi account

Account deletion is permanent. This page explains how to delete your account and what is removed.

Delete in the Kafi app (recommended)

  1. Open the Kafi app and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Select a reason, then type DELETE to confirm.

This action cannot be undone. Your profile and related data will be permanently removed.

What is deleted

  • Your profile and personal data
  • Photos, videos, and uploaded documents
  • Chat history and applications
  • Trial records linked to your account
  • Your Firebase authentication account

Families with an active subscription: cancel or manage billing in the App Store or Google Play. Platform refunds follow Apple/Google policies.

Request deletion by email

If you cannot access the app, email us from the phone number or email associated with your account:

hello@kafiapp.tech

Include: full name, phone number used to sign up, and role (nanny or family). We aim to complete verified deletion requests within 30 days.
